About

Who I Am

31 years old, originally from Romania, now living in Spain. For the past 10 years I was an entrepreneur: I owned a barbershop, ran a transportation business, and trained barbers. Four years ago I decided to make a complete career switch and dive into technology.

I moved to Spain, opened my own barbershop here, and in parallel started learning to code. What started as curiosity turned into building full automation systems, analytics platforms, and production web applications. Today I work as a Business Intelligence Engineer at Amazon, where I build enterprise tools that handle real operational data at scale.

I'm self taught. Everything you see in this portfolio was built from zero, learning along the way. From a barber chair to building 20+ tab analytics platforms with AI, I bring the same energy to tech that I brought to entrepreneurship: ship fast, solve real problems, keep iterating.

Every project here was built with AI as my pair programmer. I believe the future belongs to those who know how to leverage AI to build faster, think bigger, and ship what matters. I don't just use AI tools. I build with them, design systems around them, and push them to their limits every single day.
